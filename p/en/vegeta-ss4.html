Vegeta SS4

SSR · Defense · Light · Saiyan · Male
SS4 · Warrior DEF

Skill: MAX. Accelerated Strike

[Physical/Special Attack] Attacks a single enemy, reducing the target's ATK by 20% and Crit RES by 20%, with a 30% chance to cause [Stun] (unable to act, lasts 1 turn); considers the target of [Super Saiyan 4 Goku]'s previous skill attack as the [Combo Attack Target]. If it hits the [Combo Attack Target], the chance to cause [Stun] rises to 60%. If this skill causes Stun, raises its own ATK by 15% (2 turns), with a 60% chance to cause [Low Launch].

Supreme: SMAX. Infinite Spread

[Physical ATK/Special ATK] Attacks the enemy with the highest ATK, removes [Super Saiyan 4 Goku]'s negative effects, reduces the enemy front line's DMG Rate by 10%, raises its own DMG Reduction by 20% (1 turn) and reduces the target's Rage by 200. When [Super Saiyan 4 Goku] is present on your side of the field, it unleashes the Combined Special Strike, keeping the original effects, with DMG Reduction raised to 30%, reducing the target line's DMG Rate by 15%, and additionally raising [Super Saiyan 4 Goku]'s DMG Reduction by 15% (2 turns). On the next turn, the skill will definitely be unleashed.

Combo attack: Chase Attack

[Martial Arts / Ki Blast] Attacks a single enemy, follows up with [Knockdown], causes [Knockback]; if the target is a [Combo Target], increases damage by 50%. Deals 75%+8 damage to the target.
