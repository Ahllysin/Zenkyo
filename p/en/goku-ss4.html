Goku SS4

SSR · Attack · Lightning · Saiyan · Male
Martial Artist · SS4 · ATK Warrior

Skill: MAX. Savage Strike

[Physical ATK/Special ATK] Attacks a single enemy, reducing its DEF by 30% and its Anomaly RES by 20%, and raising Crit Rate by 20%. Considers the target of [Super 4 Vegeta]'s last strike as the [Combo Attack Target]. If it hits the [Combo Attack Target], this attack will guarantee a crit. If this strike crits, recovers 6% of its own HP and has a 60% chance to cause [Knockdown].

Supreme: SMAX. Dragon Super Punch

[Physical ATK/Special ATK] Attacks a single enemy, removes [Super 4 Vegeta]'s negative effects, reduces the enemy front line's DMG Reduction by 10%, raises its own DMG Rate by 20% (1 turn) and deals additional damage equal to 10% of the target's Max HP. If [Super 4 Vegeta] is present on your side of the field, it unleashes the combined special strike, keeping the original effects, with DMG Rate raised to 30%, reduces the target line's DMG Reduction by 15%, additionally raises [Super 4 Vegeta]'s DMG Rate by 15% (2 turns) and guarantees the skill is unleashed next turn.

Combo attack: Chase Attack

[Martial Arts / Ki Blast] Attacks a single enemy target, follows up with [Low Launch], causes [Knockback], if the target is a [Combo Target], increases damage by 50%. Deals 75%+8 damage to the target.
