Goku Superior Instinct

SSR [Lendário] · Attack · Lightning · Saiyan · Male
S Warrior · Martial Artist · ATK Warrior

Skill: MAX. Ultimate Fist

[Physical ATK/Special ATK] Attacks a single target, prioritizing the unit with the highest HP percentage in the front row. 60% chance to cause [Fallen]. If the target's HP is above 50%, damage increases by 40%. This skill permanently raises crit rate by 15% and crit strength by 15%, up to a maximum of 45%. Recovers 150 of its own rage. If its own rage is below 300, recovers an additional 150 rage. Deals 40% splash damage to targets in the back row (splash damage does not recover additional rage).

Supreme: SMAX. Explosive Heat Impact

[Special Attack] Drains 30% of the main target's ATK and DEF, raises its own Supreme DMG by 30%. Attacks all enemies (max. +120% for fewer targets). Deals 40% extra damage to [Attack] type fighters. Reduces the Crit Rate of all enemies by 12%, increased to 24% against [Attack] type fighters (1 turn). If there is a target in the enemy front row with an HP ratio below 50%, unleashes [Explosive Stellar Energy Impact]. [Explosive Stellar Energy Impact] (SMAX): drains 30% of the main target's ATK and DEF, raises its own Supreme DMG by 30%. Attacks the front-row enemy with the lowest HP ratio, applies [Supreme Suppression] to the target, ignores the target's damage cap, and deals additional damage equal to (25% of the target's lost HP + 12% of the target's Max HP). Deals 40% area damage to enemies around the target. If the main target is eliminated, area damage increases to 60%, and restores 15% of its own HP and 200 Rage. [Supreme Suppression]: reduces the target's DMG Rate against itself by 20% and Crit RES Rate by 40%, lasts 1 turn; only 1 target at a time.

Combo attack: Chase Attack

[Physical ATK] Attacks a single enemy, follows up with [Knockback], causing [High Launch]. Deals 75%+8 damage to the target.
