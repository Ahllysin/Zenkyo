Cumber

SSR [Limitado] · Attack · Lightning · Saiyan · Male
Evil Warrior · ATK Warrior

Skill: MAX. Multiple Evil Claw

[Ki] Cumber attacks a column. Per stack of [Vengeance]: +6% Crit Rate. 40% chance to [Paralyze] the main target (60% against [Nightmare]). If [Paralyzed], changes to [Stun]. +40% Life Steal. +40% damage to targets with [Nightmare], +20% to targets with [Vengeance]. With 4 stacks of [Vengeance]: guaranteed skill use. When taking heavy damage (>30% of Max HP): gains 1 [Vengeance] and marks the attacker. [Paralyze]: prevents chase attacks (1 turn).

Supreme: SMAX. Hard Evil Eraser

[Martial Arts] Cumber prioritizes attacking targets with [Vengeance]. For each stack of [Vengeance Power] it has, this attack raises its own Crit Rate by 6%. With all stacks of [Vengeance Power], additionally raises its own Crit DMG by 20%. If the target is a [Saiyan], restores 8% of its own HP. If it secures a kill, restores 300 Rage. Absorbs 25% of the main target's ATK and DEF. Against [Defense]-type warriors, ignores 35% of DEF. Enemies adjacent to the target take 30% extra damage (without generating extra Rage). Causes [Low Launch]. If the main target is not killed, it enters a nightmare state (Supreme damage dealt -25%, DEF -25%, lasts 1 turn), and Cumber's next Supreme deals 20% more damage. [Passive]: can be revived after being defeated, immediately restoring 20% of its own Max HP. Triggers once per battle.

Combo attack: Chase Attack

[Martial Arts / Ki Blast] Attacks a single enemy, follows up with [High Launch], causing [Knockback].
