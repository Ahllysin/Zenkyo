Toppo

SSR · Attack · Fire · Male
S Warrior · Pride Troopers · ATK Warrior

Skill: MAX. Tornado of Justice

[Physical ATK] Toppo attacks a single target, dealing 40% extra damage to targets with less than 50% HP; for 2 turns, has a 60% chance to reduce damage taken by 40%. Raises the chance of its own minor skills by 20%, and the lower its HP, the higher the chance, up to 100%. Has a 60% chance to cause [Low Launch].

Supreme: SMAX. Destructive Energy Sphere

[Special Strike] Attacks a single enemy, dealing 20% area damage around it. The more enemies it hits, the higher the area damage coefficient, up to 30%. If it hits 2 or more enemies, this attack cannot be blocked or resisted. Raises its own Crit Intensity by 35% for 2 turns. Deals additional damage equal to 12% of each target's lost HP. 30% of the damage is converted into its own HP. If there is only 1 enemy on the opposing field, deals additional damage equal to 15% of the highest lost HP between both sides, reduces the target's DMG Reduction by 20% for 2 turns and causes [Knockdown].

Combo attack: Chase Attack

[Martial Arts / Ki Blast] Attacks a single enemy, follows up with [High Launch], causing [Low Launch]. Deals 75%+8 damage to the target.
