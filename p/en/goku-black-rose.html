Goku Black (Rosé)

SSR · Skill · Lightning · Saiyan · Male
Evil Warrior · S Warrior · Warrior TEC

Skill: MAX. Instant Attack

[Physical ATK] Attacks a single enemy and randomly raises the ATK of 3 allied [Dark Warriors] by 10% (1 turn); from the 3rd use onward, skill damage increases by 60%.

Supreme: SMAX. Combined Gas

[Special Attack] Attacks the enemy back row, causing [Burn] on the target (at the start of each turn, takes damage equal to 25% of the applier's attack; each stack reduces healing effect by 10%, stacking up to 10 times, lasting 2 turns), and has a 40% chance to [Silence] the target (prevents Supreme skills, lasting 1 turn). Raises the Supreme skill damage of all [Dark Warriors] on your team by 8% (excluding itself; for each additional [Dark Warrior] on the team, Supreme skill damage increases by a further 3%, up to a maximum of 20%, lasting 1 turn), causing [Knockdown].

Combo attack: Chase Attack

[Martial Arts / Ki Blast] Attacks a single enemy, follows up with [High Launch], inflicts [Low Launch]. Deals 88.25%+432 damage to the target.
