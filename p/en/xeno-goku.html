Xeno Goku

SR · Defense · Light · Saiyan · Male
Martial Artist · Warrior DEF

Skill: MAX. Quick Strike

[Physical ATK] Attacks a single enemy, with a 25% chance to cause [Stun] (unable to act for 1 turn) on the target. If the target is an [Attack]-type Warrior, deals 60% additional damage and has a 60% chance to cause [Knockdown].

Supreme: SMAX. Ranger's Explosive Impact

[Physical ATK/Special ATK] Attacks a random back-row target, raises life steal by 40% and, against [Attack]-type Warriors, raises the advantage effect by 40%. When it dies, immediately reduces the enemy back row's Rage by 200 and lowers DMG Rate by 10% (2 turns), causing [Knockback].

Combo attack: Chase Attack

[Martial Arts / Ki Blast] Attacks a single enemy, follows up with [High Launch], inflicts [Knockdown]. If the target is [Stunned], restores 3% of its own HP. Deals 75%+8 damage to the target.
