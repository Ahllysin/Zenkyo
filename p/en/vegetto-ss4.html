SS4 · Fusion Warrior · ATK Warrior

Skill: MAX. Divine Void Strike

Attacks a single enemy; raises the skill's damage by 35%; deals extra damage equal to 50% of this skill's total damage to the row where the target is; with a 50% chance, reduces the rage of 1 random enemy in the target's row by 200; applies the Body Guard effect to the Warriors in its own row (on the bearer, raises damage reduction by 20%, recovers 10% of Max HP with each attack received, disappears after receiving 1-3 attacks <the more enemies on the field, the more times>, cannot be dispelled), lasting 1 turn; at the start of the next allied turn, consumes any remaining Body Guard effect and recovers 150 rage for the bearer; the character itself gets a 60% increase to the HP and rage recovery effects obtained through Body Guard.

Supreme: SMAX. Great Explosion Attack

Attacks the enemy with the highest Rage, then raises its own Damage Rate and Damage Reduction Rate by 35% and Life Steal Rate by 50% for 1 turn; all of its attacks ignore 70% of the target's Defense for 1 turn; deals up to 70% extra damage based on the target's current Rage; absorbs 200 Rage from the main target; has a 70% chance to silence the target for 1 turn; when this effect is dispelled, immediately recovers 200 Rage; removes the target's positive effects; reduces the Block Strength and Crit Strength of the target and the surrounding enemies by 35% for 1 turn; if the damage dealt by this Special Strike is less than 40% of the target's Max HP, immediately recovers all Rage; this turn, the damage of the Special Strike triggered this way will not be reduced and will not apply the Silence effect; if this Special Strike is not triggered, all Rage is reduced at the start of the turn; this effect can only be triggered once per turn.
