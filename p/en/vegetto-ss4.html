Vegetto (SS4)

SSR [Lendário] · Attack · Light · Saiyan · Male
SS4 · Fusion Warrior · ATK Warrior

Skill: MAX. Divine Void Strike

[Special Attack] Raises the skill's damage by 50% and attacks an enemy row. 50% chance to reduce the Rage of a random enemy in that row by 200. Against targets with more than 40% HP, deals extra damage equal to 10% of the target's max HP. Applies [Protection] to itself and to 1 random ally of the [Fusion Warrior] category for 1 turn. At the start of the next ally turn, consumes the remaining protection and recovers 150 Rage for each bearer. While under [Protection], the user takes at most 40% of its own max HP in damage per attack and recovers an additional 50 Rage whenever it recovers HP. [Protection]: Raises damage reduction by 30%. When it receives a skill attack or a supreme technique, recovers 5% of its own max HP. Disappears after receiving 1 to 6 attacks: the more enemies on the field, the more attacks it withstands. Cannot be dispelled or stacked. Lasts 1 turn.

Supreme: SMAX. Big Bang Attack

[Special Attack] Attacks the enemy with the most Rage. Then raises its own damage, damage reduction and crit resistance by 35%. For 1 turn, all of the user's attacks ignore 50% of the targets' defense. Deals up to 70% extra damage, depending on the target's current Rage. 70% chance to apply [Silence] to the target. Dispels the target's positive effects. Reduces the block power and crit power of the target and nearby enemies by 35% for 1 turn. If the damage of this supreme technique is below 50% of the target's max HP, immediately performs an additional supreme technique against a random enemy. That technique deals 20% less damage, but can also deal up to 70% extra damage depending on the new target's current Rage. This effect can trigger once per turn and at most three times per battle. [Silence]: prevents the use of supreme techniques for 1 turn. [Passive Effect]: when the effect that prevents death triggers, resets the number of available activations of [Special Attack]. This can happen once per battle.

Combo attack: Chase Attack

[Physical Attack] Attacks a single enemy, follows up with [Low Launch], causes [Knockdown]. For each allied [Fusion Warrior] on the field, raises this attack's Crit Rate and Crit Damage by 15%. Deals 75%+8 damage to the target.
