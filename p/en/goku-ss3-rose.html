Goku SS3 (Rosé)

SSR [Lendário] · Attack · Lightning · Saiyan · Male
Evil Warrior · ATK Warrior

Skill: MAX. Ki Scythe

[Special Attack] [Ki Blast] Attacks a random enemy row, with a 60% chance to cause [High Launch] and a 40% chance to inflict [Silence] on back-row targets. 90% chance to apply [Soul Extinguishing Flame] to the targets. Before attacking, for each [Soul Extinguishing Flame] on the enemy field, this attack's Crit Rate increases by 20%. Restores HP equal to 100% of its own ATK and 100 Rage. If it has a [Soul Extinguishing Shield], the HP and Rage restored increase by 100%. [Silence]: cannot use Supremes, 1 turn. [Soul Extinguishing Flame]: -10% ATK, damage equal to 60% of the caster's ATK per turn, 2 turns, does not stack. Enhanced [Soul Extinguishing Shield]: When the [Soul Extinguishing Shield] absorbs damage, there is a 45% chance to apply [Soul Extinguishing Flame] to the attacker. Deals 175%+15 damage to the target.

Supreme: SMAX. Black Kamehameha

[Special Attack] Raises its own DMG Rate, DMG Reduction and Life Steal Rate by 15% (2 turns). On the first use, ignores Block. Attacks an enemy row. Reduces the DEF of [Defense]-type warriors by 30% and Healing Rate by 15% (2 turns). If a target has [Soul Extinguishing Flame], deals damage equal to <10% of the target's Max HP + 180% of its own ATK>. If there is [Burn], deals additional damage equal to 90% of its own ATK. When revived, immediately deals [Special Attack] damage equal to <5% of the target's Max HP + 180% of its own ATK> to all targets. Against targets afflicted with [Soul Extinguishing Flame], raises Crit Rate and Damage by 35%. This damage does not allow recovering extra Rage. Can be triggered up to 3 times per battle. If revival attempts run out, grants itself 1 instance of [Death Immunity] (when taking a fatal blow, does not die, immediately restores 30% of its own HP, removes debuffs, permanently raises its own Supreme RES and Life Steal Rate by 25% each). The [Death Immunity] effect can only be granted once per battle. The next Supreme used after reviving ignores Block. This effect is active 3 times per battle. [Passive Effect]: Each time it revives, applies [Soul Extinguishing Flame] to 1 random enemy. Enhanced [Soul Extinguishing Flame]: reduces ATK by 15%, deals damage equal to 90% of the caster's ATK each turn, lasts 2 turns, does not stack. Deals 320%+25 damage to the target.

Combo attack: Chase Attack

[Physical ATK] Attacks a single enemy, follows up with [Low Launch], causing [Knockdown]. If the target is affected by [Soul Extinguishing Flame], this follow-up attack's damage increases by 50%. Deals 50%+8 damage to the target.
