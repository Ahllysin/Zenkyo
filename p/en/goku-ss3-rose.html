SSR [Lendário] · Attack · Lightning · Saiyan · Male
Evil Warrior · ATK Warrior

Skill: MAX. Ki Scythe

[Special Attack] [Ki Blast] Attacks a random enemy row, with a 60% chance to inflict [High Launch] and a 40% chance to inflict [Silence] on back-row targets. 90% chance to apply [Soul Extinguishing Flame] to targets. Before attacking, for each [Soul Extinguishing Flame] on the enemy field, this attack's Crit Rate rises by 20%. Restores HP equal to 100% of its own ATK and 100 Rage. If it has a [Soul Extinguishing Shield], the HP and Rage restored increase by 100%. [Silence]: cannot use Supremes, 1 turn. [Soul Extinguishing Flame]: -10% ATK, damage equal to 60% of the caster's ATK per turn, 2 turns, does not stack. Enhanced [Soul Extinguishing Shield]: When the [Soul Extinguishing Shield] absorbs damage, 45% chance to apply [Soul Extinguishing Flame] to the attacker.

[Ataque Especial] Aumenta sua própria Taxa de Dano, Taxa de Redução de Dano em 30% e Taxa de Roubo de Vida em 15% (2 turnos). No primeiro uso, ignora bloqueio.
