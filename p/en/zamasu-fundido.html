Fused Zamasu

SSR · Skill · Fire · Male
Evil Warrior · S Warrior · Fusion Warrior · Warrior TEC

Skill: MAX. Blade of Judgment

[Special Attack] Attacks a single enemy, prioritizing targets with [Judgment], dealing 60% additional damage. 2 allied Warriors have a 50% chance to recover 200 Rage. Has a 40% chance to apply [Judgment] to the target (cannot crit, lasts 1 turn), causing [High Launch].

Supreme: SMAX. Absolute Thunder

[Special Attack] Attacks 4 random enemy units, with a 40% chance to apply [Judgment] (cannot deal critical damage, cannot be blocked, can be dispelled, lasts 1 turn) to the target. Against [Saiyans], the chance rises to 60%. If the target is already under [Judgment], deals additional damage equal to 12% of the target's Max HP, causing [Low Launch].

Combo attack: Chase Attack

[Martial Arts / Ki Blast] Attacks a single enemy, follows up with [Low Launch], causing [Knockback], reduces the Rage of targets with [Judgment] by 100. Deals 75%+8 damage to the target.
