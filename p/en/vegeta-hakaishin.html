Vegeta Hakaishin

SSR [Ultimate] · Attack · Lightning · Saiyan · Male
ATK Warrior

Skill: MAX. RAGE STRIKE

Attacks a row of enemies, raises its own block rate by 40%, with a 40% chance to cause paralysis on a random enemy in the target row for 1 turn; recovers 10% of its own HP; if the main target is an Attack-type Warrior, reduces its crit strength by 20% and life steal rate by 8%; reduces damage taken from Skill-type Warriors by 40%; permanently raises its own secondary skill chance by 25% (stackable up to 3 times), with a 60% chance to cause [Knockback].

Supreme: SMAX. Gamma Burst Flash

Attacks a single enemy, absorbing 20% of damage rate and 20% of crit rate, with a 40% chance to cause paralysis; if this strike crits, deals 40% of this attack's damage as splash damage around the main target; according to the target's type, applies special effects: Attack: reduces damage rate by 15%, Defense: reduces damage reduction rate by 15%, Technique: reduces ultimate strike damage by 15%. Each time the ultimate strike is used, permanently adds one stack of "Breakthrough Potential" (each stack raises attack, defense and crit rate by 4%, stackable up to 10 times), and when counterattacking, gains an additional stack of "Breakthrough Potential". If it causes a kill while in the "Supreme Will" state, gains an additional 200 rage.

Combo attack: Chase Attack

[Physical Attack] Attacks a single enemy, follows up after [Great Launch], causes [Knockdown]; against targets with [Life Break], deals 30% extra damage.
