Gohan

SR · Attack · Fire · Saiyan · Male
ATK Warrior

Skill: MAX. Magic Explosion

[Physical ATK] Attacks a single enemy, dealing 60% extra damage to [Evil] Warriors. If there are fewer than 3 enemies, applies a 30% chance of [Stun] (unable to act for 1 turn), causing [High Launch].

Supreme: SMAX. Heaven and Earth Piercing Punch

[Special Attack] Attacks an enemy row. For each [Dark Warrior] in the target row, damage increases by 12%. If it causes an elimination, it can unleash the Supreme skill again (70% damage coefficient). The more Supreme skills the enemy unleashes in the same turn, the greater the ATK and DEF boost of this Supreme skill (max. 30%, lasting 1 turn), causing [High Launch].

Combo attack: Chase Attack

[Martial Arts / Ki Blast] Attacks a single enemy, chasing [Knockback], inflicting [Knockdown]. Deals 75%+8 damage to the target.
