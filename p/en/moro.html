Moro

SSR [Lendário] · Defense · Light · Male
Evil Warrior · Warrior DEF

Skill: MAX. Rapid Absorption

[Special Attack] Attacks a single enemy and deals 50% area damage to the surrounding enemies. This damage does not restore extra Rage. Produces additional effects according to the current [Copied Skill]: Vegeta (Rage Burst): restores 120 Rage to itself and 1 random ally. Gohan (Power Amplification): deals 50% additional damage. Piccolo (Regeneration): restores 8% of Max HP to itself and 1 random ally. Merus (Angelic Power): 40% chance to stun the main target and 20% chance to stun the surrounding enemies. [Passive Effect]: initially gains 1 additional [Copied Skill].

Supreme: SMAX. Planetary Energy

[Special Attack] Raises its own Attack by 35% and Defense by 35% for 2 turns. Attacks a single enemy and immediately gains 1 Copy Skill it does not yet have (lasts until the end of the battle). Produces additional effects based on the Copy Skills currently held: Vegeta (Rage Burst): Drains 250 Rage from the target. Gohan (Power Boost): When performing a Normal Attack, Skill or Ultimate Strike, triggers [Special Beam Cannon] against a random target, up to 3 times per turn. Piccolo (Regeneration): Drains 12% of the target's Max HP. Mers (Angelic Power): Removes buff effects from the target and removes negative effects from a random allied Warrior under Control, raising its Abnormal Resistance by 10%. When holding 4 [Copy Skills], Crit Rate permanently rises by 25%, this attack's Crit Rate rises by 100%, and gains 1 more Death Immunity effect, triggering at most 1 time per battle. [Special Beam Cannon]: [Special Attack] Attacks a single enemy, ignoring Block. This attack raises Damage Rate by 35%, Crit Rate by 35% and Life Steal by 35%, dealing extra damage equal to 15% of its own Max HP. This damage does not let the target recover extra Rage.

Combo attack: Chase Attack

[Physical ATK / Ki] Attacks a single enemy, follows up with [Knockback], causing [Knockdown].
