Moro

SSR [Lendário] · Defense · Light · Male
Evil Warrior · Warrior DEF

Skill: MAX. Rapid Absorption

[Special Attack] Attacks a single enemy and deals 50% area damage to the surrounding enemies. This damage does not restore extra Rage. Produces additional effects according to the current [Copied Skill]: Vegeta (Rage Burst): restores 120 Rage to itself and 1 random ally. Gohan (Power Amplification): deals 50% additional damage. Piccolo (Regeneration): restores 8% of Max HP to itself and 1 random ally. Merus (Angelic Power): 40% chance to stun the main target and 20% chance to stun the surrounding enemies. [Passive Effect]: initially gains 1 additional [Copied Skill].

Supreme: SMAX. Planetary Energy

[Special Attack] Raises its own ATK and DEF by 35% (2 turns). Attacks a single enemy and immediately gains 1 [Copied Skill] it did not yet have (lasts until the end of the battle). Produces additional effects according to the current [Copied Skill]: Vegeta (Rage Burst): drains 250 Rage from the target. Gohan (Power Amplification): triggers [Special Beam Cannon] on random targets with normal attacks, Skills or Supremes, up to 3 times per turn. Piccolo (Regeneration): drains 12% of the target's Max HP. Merus (Angelic Power): dispels the target's buffs, dispels the debuffs of 1 random controlled ally and raises its Abnormal Effect RES by 10%. When it has 4 [Copied Skills], permanently raises Crit Rate by 25%, raises this attack's Crit Rate by 100% and gains an additional [Death Immunity] effect (triggers up to 1 time per battle). [Special Beam Cannon]: [Special Attack] attacks a single enemy, ignores Block. This attack raises DMG Rate, Crit Rate and Life Steal Rate by 35% each, and deals additional damage equal to 15% of its own Max HP. This damage does not allow the targets to recover extra Rage.

Combo attack: Chase Attack

[Physical ATK / Ki] Attacks a single enemy, follows up with [Knockback], causing [Knockdown].
