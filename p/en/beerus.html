Beerus

SSR · Attack · Rock · Male
S Warrior · God of Destruction · ATK Warrior

Skill: MAX. Warriors' Alliance Violent Attack

[Physical ATK/Special ATK] Attacks a single enemy. During the battle, the fewer people on both sides, the greater the damage; this attack ignores block, raises damage against Defense-type Warriors by 60%, raises its own resistance to anomalies by 50% (1 turn) and has a 60% chance to cause [Knockdown].

Supreme: SMAX. Warriors' Alliance Judgment

[Physical ATK/Special ATK] Attacks the front-row enemy with the lowest HP percentage. This attack ignores block and damage resistance, and raises Crit Rate by 30%. For each allied Saiyan, Crit Intensity increases by 7%. If the target is eliminated, 70% of the excess damage is spread to all enemies (max. 2.5x its own ATK). An elimination caused by this attack prevents the target from reviving, causing [Knockback].

Combo attack: Chase Attack

[Martial Arts/Ki Blast] Attacks a single enemy, chases [Low Launch], causing [Knockdown], this attack ignores 30% of the target's DEF. Deals 75%+8 damage to the target.
