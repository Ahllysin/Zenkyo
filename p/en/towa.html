Towa

SSR [Limitado] · Skill · Fire · Female
Evil Warrior · Warrior TEC

Skill: MAX. Impact Sphere

[Special Attack] Attacks a row of enemies, with a 60% chance to cause [Knocked Down], 70% chance to cause [Charm] on the main target and 35% chance to cause [Charm] on the other targets. For each target with [Charm] on the enemy field, the damage of this skill rises by 15% (max 60%). [Charm]: Reduces Rage recovery speed by 15% and attack by 15% for 2 turns. During this period, when the target acts (except follow-ups and special skills), Towa recovers 3% of HP and 30 Rage, up to 15 times per turn. Towa cannot be affected by [Charm]. [Passive Effect]: When Towa gains [Mystic Protection], a random allied Dark Warrior also gains [Mystic Protection] with an intensity equal to 6% of Towa's HP. If Super Mira is on the field, he is prioritized and the intensity rises to 8% of Towa's HP.

Supreme: SMAX. Dark Barrier

[Special Attack] Raises Towa's attack and defense by 35%. For each target under [Charm] on the enemy field, the damage of this skill rises by 10% (max 50%). Attacks an enemy row, with a 70% chance to cause [Charm] on a random target hit. If there are no targets under [Charm] on the field, the chance rises to 100%. If the target is already under [Charm], this attack's crit rate rises by 40%. Then removes the [Control] effect (including advanced effects such as [Confusion]) from a random Warrior on her team, prioritizing the selection. If there is a target in the enemy row with HP below 40%, switches to a single-target special strike, attacking the target with the lowest HP in the enemy row, dealing extra damage equal to 25% of the target's lost HP, with a 70% chance to cause [Charm]. If the target is already under [Charm], this attack's crit rate rises by 40%. Then removes the [Control] effect (including advanced effects such as [Confusion]) from a random Warrior on her team, prioritizing the selection. Enhanced [Charm] effect: Reduces Rage recovery speed by 20% and attack by 20%. Damage dealt to the [Charmer] is reduced by 20%, and damage taken from the [Charmer] rises by 20%. Lasts 2 turns. During this period, when the target acts (except follow-ups and special skills), Towa recovers 5% of her HP and 50 Rage, up to 12 times per turn. Towa cannot be affected by [Charm].

Combo attack: Chase Attack

[Physical ATK] Attacks a single enemy, follows up with [Low Launch], causing [Knockdown].
