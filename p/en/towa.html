Towa

SSR [Limitado] · Skill · Fire · Female
Evil Warrior · Warrior TEC

Skill: MAX. Impact Sphere

Attacks a single enemy, raises its own Crit RES and Healing by 30%. +30% damage if the target is [Trapped], spreads [Trap] to a random enemy (1 turn). For 2 turns: Attack-type allies receive 40% of the damage (itself takes 60% less shared damage). When Towa's HP is below 30%, takes 100% less shared damage. Raises the Rage gained by Attack-type allies when hit by 50. Causes [High Launch].

Supreme: SMAX. Dark Barrier

[Special Attack] Raises its own ATK and DEF by 35%. For each enemy on the field affected by [Charm], this skill's damage increases by 10% (max. 50%). Attacks an enemy row. Has a 70% chance to inflict [Charm] on 1 random target hit. If there are no targets with [Charm] on the field, the chance rises to 100%. If a target with [Charm] is hit, this attack's Crit Rate increases by 40%. Then dispels the [Control] effects of 1 random allied warrior (including advanced [Control] effects such as [Confusion], with priority selection). If there is a target in the enemy row with an HP proportion below 40%, switches to a single-target Supreme, aiming at the enemy-row unit with the lowest HP proportion and dealing additional damage equal to 25% of its lost HP. Has a 70% chance to inflict [Charm]. If the target is already under [Charm], this attack's Crit Rate increases by 40%. Then dispels the [Control] effects of 1 random allied warrior (including advanced [Control] effects such as [Confusion], with priority selection). Enhanced [Charm] effect: reduces Rage Recovery Speed and ATK by 20%, reduces damage dealt to the [Charmer] by 20%, increases damage taken from the [Charmer] by 20%, lasts 2 turns. During this time, whenever the target acts (except follow-up attacks and special skills), restores 5% of Towa's HP and 50 Rage (max. 12 times per turn). Towa herself is immune to [Charm].

Combo attack: Chase Attack

[Physical ATK] Attacks a single enemy, follows up with [Low Launch], causing [Knockdown].
