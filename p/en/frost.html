R · Skill · Light · Male
Evil Warrior · S Warrior · Warrior TEC

Skill: MAX. Explosion Wave

[Special Attack] Attacks a single enemy. Deals 30% additional damage to targets already under [Poison] and reduces Rage recovery speed by 40%. Has a 60% chance to cause [Low Launch].

Supreme: SMAX. Continuous Barrage

[Special Attack] Attacks all enemies. If the target has 2 or more stacks of [Poison], deals additional damage equal to 15% of the target's lost HP. If it has 3 or more stacks, reduces the target's DMG Reduction by 15% and its Rage by 200, causing [Knockdown].

Combo attack: Chase Attack

[Martial Arts/Ki Blast] Attacks a single enemy and chases [Knockback], causing [Low Launch]. Deals 75%+8 damage to the target.
