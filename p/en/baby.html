Baby

SSR · Skill · Fire · Male
Evil Warrior · Warrior TEC

Skill: MAX. Ki Cannon Dance

[Physical ATK/Special ATK] Attacks a single target, applying [Dark Burn] (deals damage equal to 6% of Max HP per turn, lasting until the end of the battle, stackable); reduces the target's DMG Reduction by 20% and raises its own Pierce Rate by 50% (2 turns). If the target has 2 or more stacks of [Dark Burn], recovers 10% of its own HP and 200 Rage, with a 60% chance to cause [Knockdown].

Supreme: SMAX. Super Flash Cannon

[Special Strike] Attacks an enemy row, with a 65% chance to apply [Dark Burn] to the target. Deals 35% additional damage to targets that already have [Dark Burn] or [Burn]. Raises the allied front row's Block Intensity by 25%. From the third turn, it switches to the ultimate strike, attacking the enemy with the highest HP in the front row, ignoring block. Applies 100% [Dark Burn] to the target hit. Deals 35% additional damage to targets with [Dark Burn]. Raises the allied front row's Block Intensity by 35% and Block Rate by 10%. Causes [Knockdown].

Combo attack: Chase Attack

[Martial Arts / Ki Blast] Attacks a single enemy, follows up with [Knockback], causes [Knockdown]. Deals 85.5%+344 damage to the target.
