Broly

SSR · Skill · Fire · Saiyan · Male
Evil Warrior · Legendary Warrior · Warrior TEC

Skill: MAX. Legendary Strike

[Physical ATK] Attacks a single enemy, dealing 60% additional damage to Defense-type Warriors. If its own HP is below 50%, recovers HP equal to 70% of ATK. If there is only 1 enemy, damage increases by 50%, with a 60% chance to cause [Knockback].

Supreme: SMAX. Impact Explosion

[Special Attack] Attacks all enemies. The fewer the enemies, the greater the damage. Has a 30% chance to reduce each enemy's Rage by 300 and a 20% chance to cause [Stun] (unable to act, lasts 1 turn). For each [Saiyan] warrior on the enemy team, this special strike's damage increases by 6%. It has a 30% chance to revive and inherit its Rage. When there is only 1 enemy, the special strike changes: reduces the target's Rage by 500, has a 35% chance to cause [Stun], and it has a 30% chance to revive and inherit its Rage, causing [Low Launch].

Combo attack: Chase Attack

[Martial Arts / Ki Blast] Attacks a single enemy, follows up with [Knockdown], inflicting [Knockback]. Deals 87%+392 damage to the target.
