Super Syn Shenron

SSR · Skill · Fire · Male
Evil Warrior · Warrior TEC

Skill: MAX. Roll and Heavy Strike

[Special ATK] Attacks a single enemy. If the target is under [Poison], recovers 8% of Max HP and applies [Poison] to the target (deals damage equal to 4% of Max HP at the start of each turn, lasts 2 turns, max. 5 stacks). Recovers 150 Rage. Deals 40% extra damage to targets under Control. 60% chance to cause [Knockdown].

Supreme: SMAX. Super Negative Energy Ball

[Special Attack] Attacks all enemies, raises the ATK and DEF of the front row [Dark Warriors] by 7% (for 2 turns), with a 70% chance to apply [Poison] to each target. Deals 25% additional damage to all units already [Poisoned], and 20% of the damage is converted to HP for the front row. In this attack, for each negative status (Burn, Soul Burn, Poison, Bleed, Combustion) the target carries, damage increases by 8%, up to a maximum increase of 32%. Causes [High Launch].

Combo attack: Chase Attack

[Martial Arts / Ki Blast] Attacks a single enemy, follows up with [Knockback], inflicts [Knockdown], 30% chance to apply 1 stack of [Venom]. Deals 88.5%+440 damage to the target.
