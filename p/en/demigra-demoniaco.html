Demonic Demigra

SSR [Limitado] · Defense · Lightning · Male
Evil Warrior · Warrior DEF

Skill: MAX. Dragon Impact

[Special Attack] Attacks an enemy row, with a 60% chance to cause [Low Launch], and restores HP equal to 300% of its own DEF. Copies the negative statuses of the target's front row and applies them to the target's back row (cannot copy [Confusion]), and dispels the main target's buffs. If a skill was not used last turn, it will certainly use a skill next turn.

Supreme: SMAX. Descending Disaster

[Special Attack] Raises its own DMG Rate and DMG Reduction by 30%. Attacks 4 random enemy units. Has a 30% chance to inflict [Confusion] on the targets; the chance rises to 50% against [Saiyans]. If a target hit is not affected by [Confusion], deals additional damage equal to 300% of Demigra's DEF to it and reduces its ATK by 15% (2 turns). The fewer enemies hit, the greater the damage (up to a 100% increase). 40% of the damage dealt is converted into HP to heal itself. [Confusion]: cannot use Supremes or normal Skills. Forces the target to attack Demigra. After Demigra is attacked (except follow-up attacks), steals a buff from that warrior (lasts 1 turn, can only copy once from the same warrior per turn) and drains 20% of its current HP. Cannot be removed by normal dispels. Lasts 1 turn.

Combo attack: Chase Attack

[Physical ATK / Special Attack] Attacks a single enemy, follows up with [High Launch], causing [Knockback]. Deals 75%+8 damage to the target.
