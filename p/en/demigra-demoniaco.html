Demonic Demigra

SSR [Limitado] · Defense · Lightning · Male
Evil Warrior · Warrior DEF

Skill: MAX. Dragon Impact

[Special Attack] Attacks an enemy row, with a 60% chance to cause [Low Launch], and restores HP equal to 300% of its own DEF. Copies the negative statuses of the target's front row and applies them to the target's back row (cannot copy [Confusion]), and dispels the main target's buffs. If a skill was not used last turn, it will certainly use a skill next turn.

Supreme: SMAX. Descending Disaster

[Special Attack] Raises its own Damage Rate by 30% and Damage Reduction Rate by 30%. Attacks 4 random enemy units, with a 30% chance to cause [Confusion] on the target. The chance rises to 50% against [Saiyans]. If the target hit is not under [Confusion], deals Extra Damage equal to 300% of Demigra's Defense and reduces its Attack by 15% (2 turns). The fewer enemies hit, the more damage it deals, with a maximum increase of 100%. 40% of the damage dealt is converted into Life to heal himself. [Confusion]: Unable to use Special Strikes and Skills, and forced to attack Demigra. When Demigra is attacked (except by Follow-up Attacks), he steals the buff effects of that Warrior (lasts 1 turn, the same Warrior can only be copied 1 time per turn) and drains 20% of its current Life. Cannot be removed by Ordinary Dispel and lasts 1 turn.

Combo attack: Chase Attack

[Physical ATK / Special Attack] Attacks a single enemy, follows up with [High Launch], causing [Knockback]. Deals 75%+8 damage to the target.
