Gogeta SS4

SSR [Lendário] · Attack · Light · Saiyan · Male
Fusion Warrior · SS4 · ATK Warrior

Skill: MAX. Resonant Explosion

[Martial Arts/Ki] Attacks a single enemy, prioritizing targets with [Crimson Mark]. 60% chance to inflict [Low Launch], and reduces the target's DMG Reduction by 15% (2 turns). Raises its own Life Steal Rate by 25% (1 turn). Deals area damage equal to 30% of this attack's damage to the enemies behind the target. On a Critical Hit, raises this skill's damage by 40%. Deals 175%+15 damage to enemy units in a column.

Supreme: SMAX. Supreme Strike

[Ki] [Ki] raises its own ATK and Crit DMG by 30%, attacks an enemy row, reduces the Rage of all targets by 200, 90% chance to apply [Crimson Mark] to the targets hit. If a target in the row has [Crimson Mark] and does not suffer a Critical Hit, deals additional damage equal to 15% of its Max HP, inflicts [Knockback]. Gains 1 stack of [Crimson Power]. If any enemy on the field has [Crimson Mark], swaps the Supreme for [Supreme Charge]. [Supreme Charge] (SMAX): [Martial Arts/Ki] targets an enemy with [Crimson Mark], against warriors of [Defense Type] ignores Block and Damage Resistance, restores HP equal to 40% of the damage dealt and 200 Rage. If the target has [Crimson Mark] and does not suffer a Critical Hit, deals additional damage equal to 15% of its Max HP. If this skill secures a kill, gains 1 extra Death Immunity effect (does not stack), and at the start of the next turn, restores 300 Rage (triggers once per battle). [Crimson Mark]: -25% healing/-25% Crit Rate, Gogeta SS4's Crit Rate against marked targets +50% (2 turns). Deals 440%+24 damage to 3 random enemy units / Deals 320%+25 damage to the target (Supreme Charge).

Combo attack: Chase Attack

[Ki] Attacks a single enemy, chases with [Knockdown], causing [High Launch]. Deals 75%+8 damage to the target.
