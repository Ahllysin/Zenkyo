Gogeta SS4

SSR [Lendário] · Attack · Light · Saiyan · Male
Fusion Warrior · SS4 · ATK Warrior

Skill: MAX. Resonant Explosion

[Martial Arts/Ki] Attacks a single enemy, prioritizing targets with [Crimson Mark]. 60% chance to inflict [Low Launch], and reduces the target's DMG Reduction Rate by 15% (2 turns). Raises its own Life Steal Rate by 25% (1 turn). Deals area damage equal to 40% of this attack's damage to enemies behind the target. If Critical Hit, raises this skill's damage by 40%.

Supreme: SMAX. Supreme Strike

[Special Attack] Raises its own Attack and Crit Damage by 30%. Attacks an enemy row, reducing the Rage of all targets by 200. Applies [Crimson Mark] with a 90% chance to the targets hit. If the target in the row already has [Crimson Mark] and does not suffer a Critical Hit, deals extra damage equal to 15% of the target's Max HP, causing [Knockback]. It gains 1 stack of [Crimson Power]. If the enemy team has targets with [Crimson Mark], switches to the Ultimate Strike [Super Charge Attack]. [Super Charge Attack]: [Physical Attack/Special Attack] Selects a target with [Crimson Mark]. Raises its own Attack and Crit Damage by 30%. Attacks a single enemy, ignoring 30% of Defense. Reduces the main target's Rage by 200. Against enemy [Defense Type] Warriors, ignores Block and Damage Resistance. Recovers 40% of HP from the damage dealt and recovers 200 Rage. If the target has [Crimson Mark] and does not suffer a Critical Hit, deals extra damage equal to 15% of the target's Max HP. If this skill causes a kill, adds 1 Death Immunity effect (does not stack) and, at the start of the next turn, recovers 300 Rage. This effect can only be triggered once per battle. [Crimson Mark]: Reduces the target's Healing effect and Crit Rate by 25%. Super Gogeta 4's Crit Rate against targets with [Crimson Mark] rises by 50%. Lasts 2 turns. Can be removed.

Combo attack: Chase Attack

[Ki] Attacks a single enemy, chases with [Knockdown], causing [High Launch]. Deals 75%+8 damage to the target.
