Cell

SR · Defense · Rock · Android · Male
Evil Warrior · Warrior DEF

Skill: MAX. Explosive Strike

[Physical ATK] Attacks a single enemy and adds a [Shield] to itself, with an intensity of 8% of Max HP. For [Attack Warriors], reduces Rage by 200 and causes [Knockback] with a 60% chance.

Supreme: SMAX. Solar System — Kamehameha

[Special Attack] Attacks an enemy row, sacrifices 10% of its own HP to deal damage equal to 10% of the target's Max HP, with a 100% chance to cause [Burn]. When HP is below 50%, additionally raises its own DMG Reduction by 15%, Block Rate by 15% and Anomaly RES by 30% (2 turns), causing [Knockback].

Combo attack: Chase Attack

[Martial Arts/Ki Blast] Attacks a single enemy and chases [Low Launch], causing [Knockdown]. Deals 75%+8 damage to the target.
