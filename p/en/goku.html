Goku

R · Attack · Lightning · Saiyan · Male
Martial Artist · ATK Warrior

Skill: MAX. Nine-Tails Technique

[Physical ATK] Attacks a single enemy. The higher its own Rage, the greater the damage, with a maximum increase of 70%. In addition, recovers 150 additional Rage and has a 60% chance to cause [Knockdown].

Supreme: SMAX. Kamehameha

[Special ATK] Attacks an enemy row, reducing the target's Rage by 200, with a 100% chance to cause [Burn] (at the start of each turn, takes damage equal to 25% of the caster's ATK; each stack reduces healing received by 10%, up to 10 stacks, lasts 2 turns), and deals 30% additional damage to the front line, causing [Knockback].

Combo attack: Chase Attack

[Martial Arts / Ki Blast] Attacks a single enemy, chases [Low Launch], inflicts [Knockdown]. Deals 75%+8 damage to the target.
