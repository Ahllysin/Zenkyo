Kale

SSR · Defense · Rock · Saiyan · Female
S Warrior · Legendary Warrior · Warrior DEF

Skill: MAX. Crushing Strike

[Physical ATK] Attacks a single enemy, with a 25% chance to cause [Stun] (unable to act for 1 turn). Deals 60% additional damage to [Attack]-type Warriors. When there are more than 4 enemies, raises DMG Rate by 15% and causes [Knockback] with a 60% chance.

Supreme: SMAX. Ki Explosion

[Special Attack] Attacks all enemies. The fewer the enemies, the greater the damage, with a maximum increase of 120%. In addition, grants the Fury effect (increases size, recovers 20% of HP, raises DEF by 20% and DMG Reduction by 20%. During the effect, raises Anomaly RES by 90% and guarantees the minor skill is triggered on the next turn), causing [Low Launch]. If there is only 1 enemy, damage is multiplied by 3.

Combo attack: Chase Attack

[Martial Arts / Ki Blast] Attacks a single enemy, follows up with [Knockdown], causes [High Launch], deals 50% Extra Damage to male Warriors. Deals 75%+8 damage to the target.
