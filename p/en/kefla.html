S Warrior · Fusion Warrior · Legendary Warrior · Warrior TEC

Skill: MAX. Green Ki Explosion

[Special Attack] Attacks a single enemy, 100% chance to apply [Blue Flames] (reduces Supreme damage taken by 15%, the target's actions restore a small amount of Rage to Kefla, lasts 2 turns) and 1 stack of [Burn], deals additional damage equal to <60% of ATK * stacks of [Karma Flame]>. Deals 60% extra damage to units already affected by [Blue Flames]. Raises its own Debuff RES by 50% for 2 turns. Guaranteed skill use on the first turn, 60% chance to inflict [Knockdown]. This Supreme's damage increases with the number of [Blue Flames] on the field (up to +30%), causes [Knockdown]. If the target row has only 1 enemy, deals 35% additional damage. [Blue Flames]: reduces Supreme damage taken by 15%. At the start of the turn, deals damage equal to 50% of the caster's ATK.
