Full Power Jiren

SSR [Limitado] · Defense · Lightning · Male
S Warrior · Pride Troopers · Warrior DEF

Skill: MAX. Unlimited Impact

[Melee] Attacks a column, reduces the Rage recovery of all targets by 50% (1 turn). 30% of the damage is converted into HP. Gains [Energy Shield] (50% chance to reduce direct damage by 30%, dispellable, 1 turn). +40% damage to Saiyans. +25% Rage recovery (1 turn).

Supreme: SMAX. Fists of Supreme Power

[Melee] ATK/DEF scale with the Supreme skills the enemy used this turn (base 25%, max. 45%, 2 turns). Attacks the Saiyan with the lowest HP: damage scales with the enemy's Supreme skills (max. +45%). 30% chance of [Stun]. Against Saiyans: +300% of DEF as bonus damage (without generating Rage). [Passive]: when revived, gains [Energy Wall] and +15% Ki RES (2 turns).

Combo attack: Chase Attack

[Ki Blast] Attacks a single enemy, follows up with [Low Launch], inflicts [Knockback]; damage increases by 20% for each stack of [Energy Wall] on itself. Deals 75%+8 damage to the target.
