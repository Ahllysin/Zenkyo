Vegeta SSG

SSR · Attack · Light · Saiyan · Male
SSG · S Warrior · ATK Warrior

Skill: MAX. Continuous Flame Strike

[Special ATK] Attacks an enemy row. Gains bonus effects based on its own current HP: if HP is below 80%, gains 10% DMG Rate and DMG Reduction. For each additional 20% of Max HP lost, gains another 5% DMG Rate and DMG Reduction. When HP is below 50%, additionally gains 30% Rage recovery speed. If the attack hits only one target, damage increases by 40%.

Supreme: SMAX. Sacrifice Explosion

[Special Attack] Raises its own ATK and DEF by 15% (2 turns) and raises the allied back row's Crit Rate by 12% (1 turn). Attacks a single enemy. If it is Vegeta's first special strike after [Immortality], guarantees a Critical hit. If it eliminates the target, recovers 12% of its own Max HP and causes [Low Launch].

Combo attack: Chase Attack

[Martial Arts / Ki Energy] Attacks a single enemy, follows up with [Knockdown], causing [High Launch]. Deals 75%+8 damage to the target.
