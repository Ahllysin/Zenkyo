Vegeta SSGSS

SSR · Defense · Lightning · Saiyan · Male
SSG · S Warrior · Warrior DEF

Skill: MAX. Great Explosion Attack

[Physical Attack] Attacks a single enemy, absorbs 20% of the target's Block Rate, deals 35% additional damage to [Technique]-type Warriors, absorbs 200 Rage from the target and has a 60% chance to cause [Knockdown].

Supreme: SMAX. Gamma Burst Flash

[Special Strike] Attacks an enemy row, raises its own ATK by 15% and DEF by 15% (1 turn). Each time it resists or blocks damage, the special strike deals extra damage to the main target equal to 4% of its own Max HP, up to a maximum of 20%. The counter resets after using the Supreme skill. When HP is below 35%, it temporarily raises its life steal rate by 80%, changes the special strike to "Explosive Fist", dealing heavy damage to a single enemy, absorbing 20% of the target's DMG Reduction rate and 20% of its DMG Rate (1 turn), and applies [Internal Injury] to the target (reduces 6% of HP per turn and lowers Rage recovery speed by 10%, lasting 2 turns). This effect can only be triggered once per battle, causing [Low Launch].

Combo attack: Chase Attack

[Martial Arts/Ki Blast] Attacks a single enemy and chases with [High Launch], causing [Knockback], with a 30% chance to gain 100 Ki. Deals 88.5%+440 damage to the target.
