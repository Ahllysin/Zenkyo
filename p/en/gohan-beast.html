Gohan BEAST

SSR [Ultimate] · Attack · Fire · Saiyan · Male
S Warrior · ATK Warrior

Skill: MAX. Double Shockwave

[Physical ATK/Special ATK] Attacks a single enemy, with a 60% chance to cause [Knockback], gains 15 points of [Limit Power], recovers 250 of its own Rage. If its own Rage is lower than the target's, instead drains 200 Rage from the target; and drains up to 10% of the target's max HP. If the enemy's HP is above 50%, gains an additional 70%. [Limit Power]: Capped at 100 points, each point provides 0.6% Attack. [Enhanced Special Skill]: When using the special skill, additionally recovers 150 of its own Rage.

Supreme: SMAX. Hyper Special Beam Cannon

[Special Attack] Attacks a single enemy, raises its own Damage Rate by 35%, Damage Reduction Rate by 25% and Crit Rate by 25% (1 turn); if the main target is in the front row, deals 30% Splash Damage to the targets in the back row; additionally gains 15 points of [Limit Power]; each point of [Limit Power] raises this attack's Life Steal Rate by 0.3%; there is a chance to consume all [Limit Power] (each point of Limit Power gives a 1% chance) to trigger [Limit Crit]; if Limit Crit is not triggered, reduces the target's Rage by 200, reduces Attack by 25% and Crit Rate by 25% (1 turn), and gains 10 points of [Limit Power]. [Limit Power]: Maximum cap of 100 points, each point provides 0.6% Attack. [Limit Crit]: Raises this attack's damage by 70%. [Passive Effect]: [Special Skill] The per-turn limit rises to 3, and it can be triggered at most 6 times per battle.

Combo attack: Chase Attack

[Physical ATK] Attacks a single enemy, follows up with [Knockback], causing [Low Launch]. Guaranteed Critical Hit against [Evil] warriors, and gains 3 [Limit Power]. Deals 75%+8 damage to the target.
