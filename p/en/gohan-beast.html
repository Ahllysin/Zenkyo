Gohan BEAST

SSR [Ultimate] · Attack · Fire · Saiyan · Male
S Warrior · ATK Warrior

Skill: MAX. Double Shockwave

[Physical ATK/Special ATK] Attacks a single enemy, 60% chance of [Knockback], gains 15 [Limit Power], restores 250 of its own Rage (if its own Rage is lower than the target's, drains 200 Rage from the target instead). Drains 10% of the target's Max HP (drain +70% if the enemy's HP > 50%). [Limit Power]: each point grants 0.6% ATK.

Supreme: SMAX. Hyper Special Beam Cannon

[Special ATK] Attacks a single enemy, raises its own DMG Rate by 35%, DMG Reduction by 25% and Crit Rate by 25% (1 turn). If the main target is in the Front Row, deals 30% area damage to the Back Row. Gains an additional 15 [Limit Power]; each point of [Limit Power] grants 0.3% Life Steal Rate on this attack. Has a chance to consume all [Limit Power] (1% per point) to trigger [Limit Crit]; if it does not trigger, reduces the target's Rage by 200, reduces its ATK and Crit Rate by 25% (1 turn), and gains an additional 10 [Limit Power]. [Limit Power]: 0.6% ATK per point. [Limit Crit]: +70% damage. [Passive Effect]: the number of [Special Skill] uses per turn increases to 3, up to 6 times per battle.

Combo attack: Chase Attack

[Physical ATK] Attacks a single enemy, follows up with [Knockback], causing [Low Launch]. Guaranteed Critical Hit against [Evil] warriors, and gains 3 [Limit Power]. Deals 75%+8 damage to the target.
