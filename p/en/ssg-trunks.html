SSG Trunks

SSR [Lendário] · Skill · Light · Saiyan · Male
SSG · Warrior TEC

Skill: MAX. Flaming Sword Strike

[Physical ATK] Attacks an enemy row, 60% chance to cause [Knockback], raises its own Crit DMG by 20%. If there is a target with [Burn], this attack's Crit Rate increases by 40%. Deals additional damage equal to <its own Crit Rate> * ATK (max. 50% of ATK) to targets with [Burn]. If it has a [Flame Mark], consumes 1 to drain 10% of the main target's HP and 200 Rage. Enhanced [Flame Guard]: raises DMG Reduction and Crit RES Intensity by 7% * <number of enemies on the field>. Each time a Supreme is taken, this bonus decreases by 7%.

Supreme: SMAX. Cutting Hellish Explosion

[Physical ATK] Raises its own DMG Rate and DMG Reduction by 30%. Attacks an enemy row, with a 70% chance to apply [Burn]. Deals additional damage equal to <150% * its own Crit Rate> * ATK (max. 80% of ATK) to targets with [Burn]. If it has a [Flame Mark], consumes 1 to deal additional damage equal to 12% of the enemy's Max HP. If [Flame Energy] exceeds 30%, consumes 30% of it to deal additional damage equal to 30% of the [Flame Energy] consumed (divided among all targets). Stores 20% of the total damage dealt (without overflow) by this Supreme as [Flame Energy]. [Burn]: takes damage equal to 25% of the caster's ATK at the start of each turn. Each stack reduces healing effects by 10%, max. 10 stacks, lasts 2 turns. [Passive Effect]: raises the number of [Flaming Counter] uses per turn by 1. Each [Flaming Counter] raises this attack's Crit Rate by 80% and deals additional damage equal to 12% of its own Max HP.

Combo attack: Chase Attack

[Physical ATK] Attacks a single enemy, follows up with [Low Launch], causing [Knockback]. Deals 40% additional damage to targets with [Burn]. Deals 75%+8 damage to the target.
