Goku Instinct

SSR [Limitado] · Attack · Lightning · Saiyan · Male
S Warrior · Martial Artist · ATK Warrior

Skill: MAX. Quick Strike

[Melee] Attacks a single enemy. Steals 10% of HP and 200 Rage. The target takes -20% Supreme DMG and -60% Skill DMG (2 turns). 60% chance of [Knockdown].

Supreme: SMAX. Supreme Storm Shock

[Special Attack] Attacks a single enemy and absorbs 25% of the target's attack, defense, damage rate and damage reduction rate. Enemies killed by this skill have a 100% chance of not being [Revived]. On odd turns, raises Crit Strength by 15% (1 turn); on even turns, recovers 12% of Max HP and causes [Knockdown]. [Passive Effect]: When gaining [Team Boost], increases it by an additional 50% (at the start of battle, if there are more than 2 [Saiyans] on the team, raises Rage recovery speed by 30% (2 turns); if there are more than 2 [Evil Warriors], raises Crit Rate by 30% (2 turns); if there are more than 2 [Pride Troopers], gains [Unshakable] (the lower its HP, the more damage it deals, max 45%, 2 turns); if there are more than 2 [Fusion Warriors], skill chance rises by 30% (2 turns) and each skill absorbs an additional 5% of the target's HP); if it gained [Team Boost], raises damage reduction rate by 30%, life steal rate by 15% and Rage recovery by 15% (1 turn).

Combo attack: Chase Attack

[Martial Arts / Ki Blast] Attacks a single enemy, follows up with [Low Launch], causing [Knockback], and increases the chain count by 1 per round. [Ninjutsu] Attacks a single enemy, repels [Low Launch] to cause [Knockback], can be activated twice per round.
