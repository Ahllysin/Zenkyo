Broly SS4

SSR [Ultimate] · Skill · Light · Saiyan · Male
Evil Warrior · Legendary Warrior · SS4 · Warrior TEC

Skill: MAX. Energy Burst

[Physical ATK] Broly attacks a single enemy (prioritizes targets with [Blast]), 60% chance to inflict [Knockback], drains 200 Rage from the target. If it hits a [Saiyan], damage increases by a further 60%. [Passive Effect]: after [Limit Resistance], applies [Blast] to the attacker.

Supreme: SMAX. Super Burst Meteor

[Special ATK] Broly attacks a row of targets, raises its own DMG Rate and DMG Reduction by 30%, reduces the DMG Rate and DMG Reduction of the target row by 30%, restores 50% of the total damage dealt as HP to itself and a further 25% of the total damage dealt as HP to the ally with the lowest HP %. 90% chance to apply [Blast]. If this Supreme does not secure a kill, [Giant Meteor] is guaranteed to activate (max. once per turn). [Giant Meteor] has a 70% chance to be upgraded to [Supreme Giant Meteor], which, in addition to the effects of [Giant Meteor], deals additional damage equal to 80% of ATK to the target and a further 20% area damage (no Rage generated) to surrounding enemies. [Giant Meteor]: raises its own ATK and DEF by 30%, attacks a random target (prioritizes targets hit by the previous Supreme), dealing 320% of ATK as [Special Attack] damage. Raises Crit DMG by 30% against targets with [Blast], and deals additional damage equal to 15% of the target's Max HP. [Blast]: reduces all healing received by 30% and Rage Recovery Speed by 15%, lasts 2 turns. At the start of each turn, deals damage equal to 15% of Max HP to the target with [Blast].

Combo attack: Chase Attack

[Physical ATK] Attacks a single enemy, follows up with [High Launch], causing [Low Launch]. Guaranteed Critical Hit against targets with [Blast]. Deals 75%+8 damage to the target.
