Arena-Z BETA

BETA project. This is a test version: it may have bugs, crash, and change a lot between updates. Your progress can be wiped at any time. Found a problem? Let us know (link at the bottom of the page).
The game is available in Portuguese only. The app is for Android only (APK file).

Download

Download the APK (Android) Watch the Beta video

Large file: about 700 MB. Download on Wi-Fi if you can.

How to install

  1. Download Arena-Z.apk on your Android phone.
  2. Open the file. Android will ask you to allow installing apps from outside the Play Store (“unknown sources”). Allow it only for the browser or file manager you used.
  3. If Play Protect warns that the app is unknown, that is expected: the app does not come from the Play Store. Choose to install anyway.
  4. Open the game. You need an internet connection to play.

What you'll see in the Beta

These are the screens shown in the video. The game itself is in Portuguese, so the screenshots show Portuguese text.

Team Combo Table (“Tabela de Combos do Time”)

Shows your team's combo chains and the order in which each warrior chases, knocks down, pushes or launches. It has tabs for the chains, for each warrior, and an explanation of how it works.

Battle effects (“Efeitos na luta”)

A per-round panel with the buffs and debuffs active on your team and on the enemies (damage, defense, control, shield, HP and more), so you can tell what is happening in the fight.

Deploy (team positioning)

On the deploy screen you position your warriors and open the combo table without leaving it. It has Quick (“Rápido”) and Save (“Salvar”) buttons and shows your reserves.

Events and content

The Beta already has the Hero Pass, the Universe Invasion (server boss with damage goals and rewards, including a permanent avatar frame), banners such as “Lenda à escolha” (Legend of your choice) and “Ultimate à escolha”, the Hall of Fame and the shops (General, Arena, Alliance).

Top-ups and cashback

Top-ups are enabled in the Beta: the screen lists crystal packs priced in Brazilian reais. The cashback only applies when the game launches: at that point, the crystals topped up are turned into cash balance, R$ 1.90 per 60 crystals. To qualify, keep a video of the profile screen (showing the account ID) and the VIP screen, which will be requested for review.
