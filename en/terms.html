Donation terms

Last updated: 10/26/2026

In short: donating is voluntary and helps keep Zenkyo online. It does not buy items, currency or advantages in the game. On the English site, payment is made on Ko-fi, and you can ask for a refund within 7 days.

1. What the donation is

Zenkyo is a free fan project with no ads. Donations help cover the site's costs (hosting, domain and database) and keep it updated. All features of the site remain free for those who do not donate.

2. What donors receive

  • A place on the Z Alliance (supporters wall) and the Rank matching the total amount supported, unless the person chooses to donate anonymously.
  • The supporter bonus: with US$ 2 or more supported (about R$ 10), up to 5 teams published in the Community at the same time (the default is 2).
  • Nothing beyond that. A donation does not give items, currency, accounts, advantages or anything inside the game, nor the right to decide what the site will have.

3. How payment works

You choose the amount and pay on the Ko-fi page (card or PayPal). To link the support to your profile, paste the code shown on the Donate tab in the message of your donation. Zenkyo does not receive or store card or bank details. Ko-fi and PayPal fees may be deducted from the amount that reaches the project. The support appears on the wall after Ko-fi confirms the payment, which usually takes a minute or two (it may take longer for payments under review). Donations are charged in US dollars.

4. Rank and amount

The Rank is calculated automatically from the total supported and goes up when the total passes each tier's limit. The wall ranks are calculated in Brazilian reais, so donations in US dollars are converted at a fixed rate set by the site. The exact amount never appears publicly, only the Rank. The unique rank Zen'oh belongs exclusively to the site's creator and cannot be obtained by donating.

5. Refunds

  • You can ask for a full refund within 7 calendar days after payment, without having to explain why.
  • After that period, requests are reviewed case by case (for example, a payment made by mistake or twice).
  • The refund is made through the payment provider, to the same payment method. How long the money takes to come back depends on the provider and your bank.
  • When an amount is refunded, it leaves the total supported: the Rank is recalculated, the supporter bonus stops applying if the total falls below US$ 2 and, if the total reaches zero, the name leaves the wall.
  • To ask, use the contact in section 8 and include the payment reference (shown on your receipt). Do not send card details.

6. Wall, anonymity and data

When you donate, you choose to appear with your profile name or anonymously. The data we keep about the donation is described in the Privacy policy. To link the donation to your profile (and have the Rank on your profile), you need to be logged in with Discord.

7. Community and use of the site

Teams and tier lists published in the Community are the responsibility of whoever publishes them. Offensive or misleading content, or content that does not respect the rights of third parties, may be removed, and accounts that abuse the rules may lose the ability to publish. Donating does not grant immunity from these rules.

8. Contact and changes

Questions, refund requests or content removal requests: open an issue at github.com/Ahllysin/Zenkyo/issues (do not put sensitive personal data in the text). If these terms change, the date at the top will be updated. Changes do not affect donations already made.

9. No official affiliation

Zenkyo is a fan project with no affiliation with the rights holders of Dragon Ball (Bandai Namco, Toei Animation, Shueisha) or with the game's creators. Names, characters and images belong to their respective owners.
