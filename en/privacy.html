Privacy and data

Last updated: 10/26/2026

In short: Zenkyo is a free fan project with no ads and no tracking or visitor analytics tools. Without logging in, nothing you build leaves your browser. If you log in with Discord, we only store what is needed for your profile, the Community and syncing — and you can delete all of it whenever you want.

1. Without logging in

Collection, teams, lineups, tier list, favorites and preferences stay only in your browser (the device's local storage). We do not send any of this to a server. If you clear your browser data, it is gone.

2. Logging in (Discord)

Login is done through Discord, using the Supabase service. When you log in, we store:

  • Login account data: account identifier, Discord display name and picture (avatar), and the email of your Discord account (passed on by Discord and kept by the login service; it is not shown on the site and not used for contact or advertising).
  • Synced data: collection, teams, lineups, tier list and favorites, so you can see the same content on another device.
  • Profile (optional): nickname, server, introduction, Instagram, Twitch, YouTube, rank form/color. Other people only see this data if you mark the profile as public; social networks only appear if you also check show socials.
  • Community: teams and tier lists you publish (title, description, content and author name), plus your likes and reports. Published posts are public.

3. Support (Z Alliance)

  • Donations are not open on the English site yet. When they open, payment will be made on the payment provider's own page, and Zenkyo will not receive or store card or bank details.
  • To record the support, we store the amount, the date, the payment reference and, depending on your choice: your account (if you are logged in with Discord), the name you type for the wall, or nothing (anonymous donation, kept off the wall).
  • The wall ranks are calculated in Brazilian reais, so donations made in US dollars are converted at a fixed rate set by the site (the converted amount is stored together with the original one).
  • Publicly, only the rank (tier) appears, never the exact amount; the exact amount is only visible to the supporter and to the administrator.

The site's fonts and libraries are served by the site itself: we do not use Google Fonts, CDNs or trackers. The "Describe the team" feature runs in your browser: the text you type is not sent anywhere.

5. Browser storage

We use the browser's local storage only for the site to work (saving your choices and keeping you logged in). We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

6. Your rights

  • Delete your online data: in the Profile tab, the Delete my data button removes your profile, your Community posts and likes and your synced data from the server. Data stored on your device stays with you.
  • Deleting the login account entirely (including the email kept by the login service), correcting or viewing data, or removing your name from the supporters wall: ask using the contact below.
  • You can make your profile private at any time (by unchecking "Appear…" in Profile) and delete your posts in the Community itself.

7. Security

The site uses HTTPS, a content security policy (CSP) that blocks third-party scripts and sending data to other addresses, and database rules so each person can only change their own data. No system is 100% secure, but we take this seriously.

8. Children and teenagers

Zenkyo is not aimed at children. Login depends on Discord, which has its own minimum age. If you believe a minor's data was sent by mistake, contact us to have it removed.

9. About the project

Zenkyo is a fan project with no official standing and no affiliation with the rights holders of Dragon Ball (Bandai Namco, Toei Animation, Shueisha) or with the game's creators. Names, characters and images belong to their respective owners. If you own any content and want it removed, use the contact below and we will remove it.

Donations (Z Alliance) only keep the site online. They do not buy items, currency or advantages in the game.

10. Contact and changes

Questions or requests about your data: open an issue at github.com/Ahllysin/Zenkyo/issues (do not put sensitive personal data in the text). If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated.
